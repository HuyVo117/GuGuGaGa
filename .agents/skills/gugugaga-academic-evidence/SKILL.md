# Academic evidence without fabrication

## Authority
- Read and follow actual Project-Guidelines and assignment book; do not invent rubric facts when files are missing.
- GuGuGaGa is Pathway 1, an inherited codebase. Distinguish historical product development from activities performed during this course.
- Never create fake signatures, approvals, retrospective minutes, dates, bugs, risks, commits, burndown or change requests.

## Evidence inventory
Validate presence and quality of:
1. Final Technical Report (15–25 pages / 7 chapters where instructor guideline confirms).
2. Signed Project Charter.
3. WBS and WBS Dictionary with 100% Rule.
4. Risk Register Evolution, real Sprint 1 and Sprint 2 snapshots.
5. Sprint 1 and final Retrospectives with action owners.
6. Actual Scope Change Control Record with impact and authentic CCB decision.
7. Stakeholder Acceptance Sign-off from designated lecturer, if accepted.
8. Presentation Slides and demonstrable software, plus approved link-sharing policy.

## Traceability audit
- Requirement -> WBS -> Issue -> PR/commit -> test -> deployed increment -> stakeholder feedback.
- Every assertion in the final report about 'completed', 'tested', 'approved', 'deployed' requires a verifiable source, otherwise write Pending, Planned or Not evidenced.
- Report real variance: planned vs actual, reasons, response and revised forecast; do not smooth adverse outcomes.
- Review privacy and remove private tokens/personal details from submitted screenshots/logs.

## Output
Evidence matrix with PASS / MISSING / INCOMPLETE / BLOCKED, exact source links, owner, due date and suggested remediation. Treat signed items as external human actions, not AI-generated artifacts.
